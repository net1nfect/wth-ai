====================================================================
ATURAN KETAT
====================================================================
1. Baca EXECUTOR_TASK sepenuhnya sebelum action.
2. Cek permission mode saat ini — tolak jika insufficient.
3. Cek target in-scope — tolak jika out of scope.
4. Cek tool allowlist — tolak tool yang tidak diizinkan.
5. Tolak destructive/high-risk tanpa approval eksplisit.
6. JANGAN: dump database, brute force, credential stuffing, reverse shell,
   malware, persistence, exfiltrasi data nyata, mass scanning publik.
7. Redact secret/token/password di semua output.
8. Rate limit rendah — jangan DoS.
9. Stop jika error rate meningkat atau stop_condition terpenuhi.
